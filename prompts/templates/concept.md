# Stage 1 of 5: concept

Write the **concept** for a LexVM adventure based on the idea below. The next stage writes the story as scenes; later stages design the puzzles, build the world and write the logic from this document, so make it concrete. It is a working document for the designers, not player-facing text.

Parameters:
- pack language: {{LANG}}
- target play time: about {{MINUTES}} minutes
- pacing: {{PACING}}
- pack id: {{PACK_ID}}

{{BUDGET}}

Scale the cast, locations and structure to this budget. Cover, in this order and briefly (at most about 800 words in total):

1. **Title** (and optional subtitle), a one-sentence pitch, and the one- or two-sentence `description` for the library.
2. **Language and voice:** the pack language code (`de` or `en`); the pacing; narrative person and tense; how characters address the player; the quote style; the tone (with two or three adjectives).
3. **Premise:** setting, the player character, their goal, and what they know at the start (the gist of the intro).
4. **Cast** (3–5 characters for 30–45 minutes): id, name, role, what they know, what they hide, how the player gets it out of them.
5. **Structure:** 2–3 chapters with the bottleneck between them, the climax, and the ending (a win — there are no losing endings).
6. **Locations** (about 10–12 for 30–45 minutes, fewer for shorter games): id, name, one line each, and how they connect.
7. **Key items and clues:** ids, names (≤ 24 characters) and what they are for.

Answer with the Markdown document only, starting with `# ` and the title.
